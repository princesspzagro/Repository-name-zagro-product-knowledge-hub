Water-soluble support

Electrolytes and nutrients supplied in drinking water may support hydration and nutrient intake. Results depend on the formula, dose, water quality, and animal condition.

Probiotics & enzymes

Selected microorganisms may support microbial balance; enzymes act on specific substrates. Do not assume every product contains the same organisms or enzymes.
